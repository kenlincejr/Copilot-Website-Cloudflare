## 2. MISA — the bar rose materially in FY27

**Current services-partner path** [MS-OFFICIAL, microsoft.com/en-us/security/business/intelligent-security-association, fetched 2026-08-28]:

1. Enrolled as Security Solutions Partner in MAICPP
2. Security designation with **4 Security specializations**
3. **≥6 Frontier Accelerate for Security Envisioning & POC workshops** delivered via MCI in the last 12 months
4. **≥1 managed security service offer** published to Microsoft Marketplace
5. **Nomination only** — no open application

**Requirements drift explains why sources conflict** — different vintages, not disagreement:

| Criterion | Deck ~2024 | Deck ~2025 | Live FY27 page |
|---|---|---|---|
| Security specializations | ≥1 | ≥2 | **4** |
| GTM engagement | CSI program | MCI, CPOR Managed Services | **≥6 Frontier Accelerate workshops/12mo** |

⚠️ Any pre-July-2026 source saying Security specializations validate on "3 customer references, no direct cost" is **now wrong** — all four moved to partner-funded biennial third-party audit on 2026-07-30.

**Perverse detail:** the Security specialization category caps benefits at **three**. MISA requires **four** — the fourth earns zero incremental benefits.

**Positioning, from Microsoft's own deck:** "MISA is the **marketing channel** for strategic security partners." It is co-marketing, not lead generation. Scale: 330+ orgs, 260+ integrations, 1,300+ security service offers.

**VERDICT: IGNORE.** Nomination-only means you cannot self-apply, and a six-workshops-per-year cadence presupposes a Microsoft field relationship a 5–50 person firm does not have.

**The reachable rung instead — Solutions Partner for Security, SMB track** [MS-OFFICIAL, solutions-partner-security, updated 2026-07-31]. Microsoft scores both Enterprise and SMB paths and **takes the higher**. Steps 1 and 2 are mandatory gates — zero Step-3 points without them.

### MISA "SMB Verified" — read this correctly

Launch holders (Blackpoint Cyber, Huntress, Sophos MDR) are **all security software vendors, not MSPs**. It marks *products sold to MSPs*, and it is gated on prior MISA membership. **Use it as a procurement filter** — a Microsoft-engineering-reviewed signal that an MDR product is built for 5–300-seat tenants — not as a status to chase.

### Verified MXDR

**The dependency runs opposite to the common assumption: MISA membership is a prerequisite *for* MXDR verification, not a reward from it.** Requires a genuine 24×7×365 SOC, proactive threat hunting, IR, posture management, threat intel, managed SIEM, with coverage beyond endpoint into identity, cloud apps, email and cloud SIEM. **81 partners worldwide as of June 2026** [COMMUNITY, Corsica Technologies, 2026-06-09] against ~15,000 in the security ecosystem.

**Free on-ramp worth taking regardless:** Microsoft publishes an **MXDR Roadmap self-assessment** — ~5 hours, personalised gap-closure recommendations, re-scorable, no status required. [MS-OFFICIAL, learn.microsoft.com/en-us/assessments/e7a8064f-657e-45f3-8a3a-c3c22322dc09/]

**VERDICT: IGNORE the status; white-label an existing MXDR Verified provider to reach the same customer outcome.**

## 3. FastTrack — live in FY27, and the customer benefit just improved

**FastTrack Ready (the partner program) is NOT retired.** No deprecation notice in FPC or Partner Center announcements Mar/May/Jun/Jul 2026; eligibility doc actively maintained (updated 2026-08-18). **But the money moved:** FY27 Copilot vouchers now gate on **Copilot Jumpstart Ready Tier**, not FastTrack Ready.

**Two-stage entry** (best source: FY25 FPC Program T&Cs, 2025-02-10 — an FY26 revision exists but is not machine-retrievable, so these may have moved):

- **FPC Program Partner:** active MAICPP + ≥1 specialization from a closed list, **or** membership in CSI, Meeting Room, Teams Phone Operator, Jumpstart, or Viva Glint. **Must be invited. Hard cap: 700 partners.**
- **FastTrack Ready opt-in:** agree to deliver the FastTrack Benefit **at no additional customer cost**, claim via CPOR, complete training. **Hard cap: 400 partners.**

**The retention bar is the disqualifier** — trailing-12-month usage growth in ≥1 category: 2,500 MAU (Intune / O365 / Security / Identity / Teams Phone), 1,000 MPU (Purview / Teams Rooms), 500 MAU (Viva), plus ≥1 new CPOR claim per month on average.

**Do partners get paid for FastTrack? No.** "Participation in this Program and any benefit opportunity shall be at Program Partner's cost… Microsoft shall in no way be liable for the difference between the payments received and the Program Partner's costs."

**FY27 QTM voucher catalog** [MS-OFFICIAL, fpc.microsoft.com KB articles]:

| Program | Minimum | Payment | CSP eligible? |
|---|---|---|---|
| M365 E3/E5/E7 Migration & Deployment (KB-01674) | Enterprise <1,000 E3 | 50/50 | ❌ CSP + SMB excluded |
| M365 Copilot Voucher (KB-01831) | **500 seats** | 50/50 | ❌ CSP excluded |
| Copilot Agents Voucher (KB-01841) | not stated | 30/70 | ❌ CSP excluded |
| Business Central Migration (KB-01842) | >$25k ACV | 30/70 | ✅ **CSP + SMB included** |

**Resolves the dossier 02/03 conflict:** the Copilot voucher floor rose **200 → 500 seats** for FY27 (two live fetches say 500; a cached snippet says 200). **Use 500.**

**Microsoft-delivered FastTrack — the 150-seat floor, with one real improvement** [MS-OFFICIAL, learn.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-365/fasttrack/eligibility, updated 2026-08-18]:

- 150+ eligible paid licenses per tenant. **Business Premium is an eligible plan.** Copilot assistance additionally needs ≥1 Copilot license.
- Covers Purview (DLP, DSPM, IRM, Audit, Comms Compliance, Compliance Manager, DLM, eDiscovery), Entra, Intune + full Intune Suite, Defender XDR, Windows 365, and **Microsoft Agent 365**.
- 🔴 **NEW: data migration floor dropped from 500 to 150** [MS-OFFICIAL, /fasttrack/data-migration, updated 2026-08-21]. Multiple older sources still say 500 — resolve in favour of the newer doc. Sources supported: on-prem Exchange hybrid, Google Workspace, SMB file shares, Box, Dropbox.

**VERDICT: LATER for FRP membership. PURSUE the customer benefit now** — submit FastTrack RFAs for every customer at 150+ licenses. The nearer-term prize is **Copilot Jumpstart Ready Tier**, which gates FY27 Copilot money.

## 4. Solution Assessments — the finding that reverses the usual advice

**Status:** the Learn doc is live but 15 months stale (ms.date 2025-05-28). "Solution Assessment" appears **zero times** in the full text of both June and July 2026 Partner Center announcements (8,423 + 11,155 words, grepped). Also dropped as a Partner of the Year category. Best reading: **unretired-but-unfunded legacy plumbing**; the funded motion moved to **Frontier Accelerate** delivered via MCI engagements.

**FY27 funded engagement caps** (term 2026-07-01 → 2027-06-30). Market A = 100%, B = 75%, C = 50%; headline caps are maxima at the largest tier:

| Program | Pre-sales (Envisioning & PoC) | Post-sales (Deployment Accelerator) |
|---|---|---|
| Copilot | up to $25K | up to **$50K** |
| AI-Ready Productivity | up to $25K | up to $40K + Conversion Bonus |
| Security | up to $15K | up to **$50K** + Conversion Bonus |

### 🔴 SMB is split down the middle

Verbatim from a distributor's FY27 rendering, slide "SMB PATH · SPD PARTNERS":

> ✕ No Pre-Sales incentives · ✕ No Cloud Accelerate Factory access · ✓ **Post-Sales funding only** · ✓ Azure Credits via Co-Sell

[CHANNEL-PRESS, Crayon ANZ FY27 Partner Briefing p35, 2026-07-17; corroborated independently by softspend.com, 2026-08-04]

- **Pre-sales carries a 300+ seat floor**; SMB is "co-op only."
- Microsoft **removed the historical $1M ACR threshold**: FY27 SMB Path = 80%+ of customers in SMB/Corporate segment regardless of ACR.
- **Deployment accelerators gate on Solutions Partner *designations*, not specializations.**

**Funded SMB post-sales engagements that fit a 25–300 seat book** (Market A, XXS tier):

| Engagement | Seat floor | Fee |
|---|---|---|
| **CSP Business Premium Defender/Purview Suites Deployment Accelerator · SMB** | **50+ BP suite seats** | **$2,000** |
| CSP ME5/ME7 Deployment Accelerator | 50+ | $3,000 |
| Conversion Bonus BP (SMB) | competitor displacement | $1,600 |
| Conversion Bonus ME5/ME7 | 50+ | $2,400 |

**Mechanism is reimbursement at risk, not a grant.** MCI claim lifecycle: customer claimed → awaiting consent → consent received → draft → submitted → under review → Approved/Rejected/**Rejected final**. Requires proof of execution plus customer attestation. **The partner fronts delivery cost and can lose the claim.** [MS-OFFICIAL, incentives/mci-engagements, ms.date 2025-12-03]

### "Microsoft-funded assessment" — six spin markers

1. "Funded by Microsoft. Delivered by [partner]" with no caveats — omits seat minimums, market tier, rejection risk.
2. "Free assessment" pitched to an SMB prospect — **below 300 seats there is no Microsoft pre-sales money.**
3. "Microsoft-funded" on SMB work — in practice almost always **co-op**, i.e. the partner spending its own earned 40% accrual. Co-op also caps proof-of-value work at 5%.
4. "ECIF-funded" with no named field nomination — no seller, no ECIF.
5. **"Advanced Specialization" or "Gold competency" in current copy** — both retired terms; a reliable tell the page predates 2023.
6. Headline caps ($25K/$50K) quoted as the expected amount.

**ECIF, verbatim:** "**ECIF Funding is a Microsoft field-led investment, not partner-led.**" [MS-OFFICIAL, techcommunity, Microsoft Community Manager, 2025-07-28] SMB/scale accounts have no assigned AE to originate a nomination — structural, not a written policy bar.

**Free tooling that IS reachable:** **ASPX** in Partner Center — Copilot tab with a **Data Security Maturity** column, identifies Copilot-ready tenants and "maps pre- and post-sales funded program eligibility to each customer," plus a Data Security storytelling playbook (`aka.ms/ASPXDataSecurityPlaybook`). Also **CloudAscent** targeting and **Azure Migrate Collector**.

**VERDICT: IGNORE pre-sales / PURSUE post-sales.** The BP Defender/Purview Deployment Accelerator at **$2,000 on a 50-seat floor, gated on the Security designation you are already pursuing**, is the single most reachable piece of Microsoft delivery money in this entire research set. Market discovery work as "assessment built on Microsoft's readiness data" — never as "Microsoft-funded."

## 5. Training: Learning Partner / MCT

Legacy "Microsoft Learning Partner" → **Training Services Partner (TSP)** (base tier, open) + optional **Solutions Partner for Training Services** designation ($4,875/yr).

**Base TSP requirements** [MS-OFFICIAL]: active MAICPP · official courseware via Microsoft Learn · deliver all courses via MCTs · achievement code + **Metrics that Matter (MTM)** survey per student. No minimum MCT headcount, delivery count, revenue commitment, or business plan. Onboarding ~1–2 weeks. No fee published (absence ≠ $0).

**Designation criteria** (changed 2025-08-01), trailing 12 months: **1,000+ courseware points** · **200+ exam units** (must be bought via **Pearson Partner Store**) · **MTM average ≥4.25/5.00**. That is a full-time training company's output. **IGNORE.**

**MCT individual** [MS-OFFICIAL, credentials/certifications/mct-enrollment, updated 2026-07-06]:

- One qualifying certification + **AB-730 or AB-731 on your Learn profile** (hard AI gate) + agreement + fee.
- **Instructional-skills model changed:** the old bring-your-own train-the-trainer cert is gone, replaced by a **3-day MCT Onboarding Experience** taught by existing MCTs. Microsoft writes "(fees apply)" and **never publishes the amount**.
- **Fee: $400 USD** new and renewal, reinstated 2025-07-01. Third-party "$150–$1,000" ranges are unattributable.
- **MCT Alumni** tier for MCTs in good standing who stop training — no annual fee, retains Lounge and exam discounts.

⚠️ **Two conflicting official renewal rules.** learn.microsoft.com (updated 2026-08-21) says 6 MTM-recorded deliveries and explicitly permits custom/private content. The Tech Community MCTRenewReq.pdf (effective 2026-10-01) says LDC/Product-Group courseware **only**, *and* requires TSP representation. Opposite answers on the one question that matters to an MSP. **Verify with mctprog@microsoft.com.**

**Funding motions:** **SATV is DEAD** (ended 2022-01-01). **ESI is closed both ways** (EA+SA customer benefit, invite-only, no published partner application route). **Copilot adoption/change management is where the money is, and it carries zero MCT/TSP/ESI gating** — free partner skilling exists via **M365 Copilot SMB Learning Journeys** on the Partner Skilling Hub and Project Ready Workshops.

Market signal: Kaseya 2026 — 48% of MSPs say AI is clients' top need, only 13% call it a meaningful revenue source.

**VERDICT: LATER (one MCT seat at most) / IGNORE the designation. PURSUE Copilot adoption as a service line without the training apparatus.**

## 6. Device / Surface

**Structural reset.** Surface Commercial Channel Simplification (Dec 2024): **Surface Hub is no longer a separate authorization** (VADOR gone); one authorization covers Surface PC + Hub + accessories. **Legacy DMP and VADOR agreements expired 2025-09-30 and are NOT renewable.** Both ADR and DMP are now **evergreen**, onboarding via Partner Center click-to-accept. Verify at Account Settings → Agreements and → Legal Info → Devices.

**Penalty for lapsing:** "if a reseller's authorization expires, they will not be able to access Pricing Programs or earn Channel Incentives."

**ADR vs DMP:** "Acceptance of **either** the DMP authorization or Surface ADR authorization is required for all resellers to be authorized by Microsoft to sell Surface products." Only ADR-exclusive benefit: **Platinum tier at $30M TTM (US)**.

⚠️ **DMP authorization ≠ a purchasing relationship:** "Grant of DMP authorization by Microsoft doesn't guarantee that an ADD will be willing to establish a relationship with you."

**Surface Reseller Alliance tiers** (FY26 term; FY27 not yet published) — Category 1 (US/UK/Japan) TTM MSRP: Bronze **$500K** · Silver $1M · Gold $2.5M · Platinum $30M. Surface Hub excluded from these insights.

**Distributor nuance:** TD SYNNEX Canada advertises a "Breadth Level — no authorization required" tier with local promos but **no deal registration and no warranty attach**. A distributor will sell you boxes unauthorized; you just get none of the Microsoft-side economics. Note "over 50% of Surfaces are sold with an attached warranty" — attach is a real margin lever gated behind authorization.

**No Copilot+ PC-specific partner incentive program found** in any Microsoft or channel source. It is a marketing category, not an incentive SKU.

**VERDICT: PURSUE DMP** (free, evergreen, click-to-accept, minimum gate for pricing programs and incentives). **IGNORE ADR.**

## 7. Segment authorizations

### Education — **no partner authorization exists** (contradicts channel folklore)

The load-bearing fact, from Microsoft's own reseller/customer qualification matrix [MS-OFFICIAL, partner-center/get-special-pricing-for-offers]:

| Special pricing | **Reseller required qualifications** | Customer |
|---|---|---|
| Education | **None** | Qualified Education User |
| Nonprofit | **None** | Sign up via nonprofit program |
| GCC Moderate | Direct CSP Partner Org form | Qualified US Gov entity |
| GCC High / DoD | Azure Gov validation form; **"only available upon invite"** | Qualified US Federal entity |

The current education selling guide (`/partner-center/customers/sell-to-education-customers`, **updated 2026-04-28 — the freshest doc in this set**) walks the full CSP EDU motion and **never mentions AEP, an exam, an application, or a partner authorization.** Every gate is customer-side.

**Source conflict, resolved by dating:** third-party pages asserting AEP is mandatory trace to a **2017**-dated page and undated marketing copy; TD SYNNEX's "CSP for EDU" resource page now 404s. **Assessment: AEP is a legacy academic-volume-licensing authorization; it does not gate CSP EDU today.**

**The real work is customer qualification:** Partner Center → Add Customer → Special qualifications = Education. Needs legal entity name matching official records, non-free email domain (gmail/hotmail auto-reject), K-12 vs Higher Ed segment. Review typically ≤3 business days. 🔴 **Hard cap: 3 verification attempts per customer, lifetime.** Corrections must be made in **M365 Admin Center**, not Partner Center.

**Copilot for Education** [MS-OFFICIAL, updated 2026-08-18]: **Teach in the Copilot App** = Faculty/Staff only, explicitly not students. **Learning Activities** = Faculty/Staff *and* Students, enabled by default, **org-wide toggle only — cannot scope to users or groups**. Age gate 13+.

**VERDICT: PURSUE.** No authorization, no exam, no fee. Just get the first verification submission right.

### Government

**GCC (moderate): PURSUE via your distributor.** 🔴 Critical for indirect resellers: "**GCC qualification applies to Indirect Provider and direct-bill partner types**" — you don't personally get GCC-qualified; **your distributor's tenant carries it and transacts.** Your customer's intake form needs the *provider's* tenant ID, not yours. Process: `usgovintake.embark.microsoft.com` — "This form constitutes a legally binding contract." Response within two business days. $0 fee. Do NOT use an Azure GCC Tenant ID. If no validation ID appears at customer creation, **stop** — post-creation GCC qualification can only be changed via API.

**GCC High / DoD: IGNORE.** Microsoft's own how-to-buy table lists **CSP for GCC but NOT for GCC High**; DoD is EA-only [MS-OFFICIAL, updated 2025-12-02]. The only sub-500-seat path is **AOS-G**, and Microsoft publishes the ~60-name partner list (**Pax8 and TD SYNNEX are both on it**) but **no application process, fee, or timeline**. **If a client needs it, subcontract to an existing AOS-G partner.**

🔴 **CMMC timing — the demand driver just got pulled.** On **2026-07-13** the Department of War issued memoranda **immediately suspending CMMC Phase II** (memo signed 2026-07-10 by CIO Kirsten Davies), also halting Phase III/IV milestones, with a 60-day program review. Phase II would have taken effect 2026-11-10. Confirmed via 8+ independent law-firm alerts (Holland & Knight, Jenner & Block, Crowell, Wiley, Morrison Foerster) plus Breaking Defense; war.gov 403s to bots. **Still in force:** Phase I, CMMC L1/L2 self-assessment, DFARS 252.204-7012, NIST 800-171 Rev 2, SPRS posting. **Do not build a GCC High practice thesis on a CMMC deadline right now.** The 60-day review concludes ~mid-September 2026 — recheck.

**Does the MSP itself need CMMC certification? NO.** Under the final rule "it is not required for ESPs to obtain their own certification" — ESP services are assessed inside the customer's own assessment. **This reversed the proposed rule; do not rely on pretrained memory.** Exception: an ESP that is a **CSP handling CUI must meet FedRAMP Moderate** (or DoD equivalency).

### Nonprofit — PURSUE, but sell services not licenses

No partner authorization, no fee. Customer validated by a **third party**: **Goodstack** became the validation partner **2025-09-23** (TechSoup ceased reviews ~Aug 2025 [UNVERIFIED]). **Timing conflict:** Learn says "up to 3 business days," the nonprofit FAQ says "up to seven" — **quote 7 days**.

🔴 **FY26 grant changes, confirmed and significant** [MS-OFFICIAL, /industry/nonprofit/microsoft-for-nonprofits/updates]:

- **M365 Business Premium grant DISCONTINUED** at next renewal on/after **2025-07-01**
- **Office 365 E1 grant DISCONTINUED**, same trigger
- Replacement: up to **300 granted Business Basic** licenses + up to 75% discounts
- **No automatic migration** — transitions must be completed **manually** in the M365 admin center

**Economics are inverted:** the anchor SKU (Business Basic ≤300 seats) is a **$0-margin grant**; Business Premium is $5.50/user/mo (75% off). Microsoft's own guidance: "Education and nonprofit tend to have lower or no margins for CSP partners."

**Two recurring obligations that are billable work:** the **85% active-usage rule** (failure = program abuse, Microsoft may reduce access) and a periodic donor-rules attestation. On qualification removal: **90-day notice, auto-renewal disabled, and you lose management rights**.

**Cross-segment mechanics:** GCC customers can buy **only** GCC. Nonprofit and Education customers can also buy Commercial. Reduced pricing never applies to software subscriptions or Azure in any segment.
