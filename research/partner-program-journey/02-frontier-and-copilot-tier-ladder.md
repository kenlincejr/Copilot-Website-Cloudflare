# The Frontier Constructs and the Copilot Partner Tier Ladder

**Research dossier — 02 · Partner Program Journey (Zero to Frontier)**

Tag legend: `[MS-OFFICIAL]` = learn.microsoft.com / partner.microsoft.com / Microsoft-authored, with URL and date · `[CHANNEL-PRESS]` = reputable channel media / distributor blogs · `[COMMUNITY]` · `[UNVERIFIED]`.

## 0. Naming disambiguation (critical)

There are TWO unrelated "Frontier" things plus a badge family:

- **Frontier Partner badge / Frontier Partner specialization** — B2B partner-program credentials in MAICPP. The subject of this dossier.
- **"Microsoft Frontier" / Microsoft 365 Copilot Frontier program** — a CUSTOMER-facing early-access program: tenant-level opt-in giving M365 Copilot-licensed customers (and Premium/Personal/Family consumers) pre-GA AI features and experimental agents; admin-managed per user/feature; **Copilot Cowork shipped through Frontier**, so Frontier governance gates agentic-work features. [MS-OFFICIAL: https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-365/admin/manage/get-started-frontier ; https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-365-copilot/frontier-program ; June 2026 Partner Center announcements ("For Frontier customers currently using Cowork, set up usage-based billing… by June 30, 2026")] Do not conflate: the partner badge is earned via designations/specializations; the customer program is a license + admin opt-in.
- **Frontier Transformation Engineer badge** — an INDIVIDUAL practitioner badge (Titan Academy learning path for solution engineers/architects: certifications → Project Ready delivery → advanced governance readiness), distinct from the company-level partner badge. [MS-OFFICIAL: https://blogs.microsoft.com/blog/2026/04/21/accelerating-frontier-transformation-with-microsoft-partners/ ; techcommunity blog 4528635 (title-render only)]

## 1. Frontier Partner BADGE (current, retiring)

- **What it is:** Company-level recognition badge (not a designation, not a specialization) in MAICPP for partners with "advanced AI driven capabilities" in the agentic-AI era; "AI-first, human-led" positioning. [CHANNEL-PRESS: Slalom, HSO, Insight press pages]
- **Launch:** Unveiled at **Microsoft Ignite 2025** (Nov 2025), alongside the Frontier Distributor designation and Support Services designation. Insight named among first recipients. [CHANNEL-PRESS: Sherweb Ignite 2025 recap https://www.sherweb.com/blog/partner/microsoft-ignite-2025-recap/] — this matches the playbook's frontier.html ("introduced at Microsoft Ignite in November 2025").
- **How earned — criteria-based, NOT invitation-based.** Requirements (per Slalom's Mar 26, 2026 announcement, the most precise public statement):
  - **3 Solutions Partner designations:** (a) AI Business Solutions (formerly Business Applications), (b) Security, (c) a Cloud & AI Platform–aligned designation.
  - **3 specializations:** (a) Microsoft (365) Copilot, (b) AI Apps on Azure OR AI Platform on Azure OR Accelerate Developer Productivity, (c) Data Security.
  [CHANNEL-PRESS: https://www.slalom.com/us/en/who-we-are/newsroom/slalom-earns-microsoft-frontier-partner-badge (earned 2026-03-26)]
  Note vs playbook: frontier.html lists the specialization ② as "AI Apps on Microsoft Azure" only — the Slalom statement says AI Apps OR AI Platform OR Accelerate Developer Productivity satisfies it, and designation ① as AI Business Solutions (Business Applications), not "Modern Work or Business Applications." Worth reconciling on the page.
- **No co-sell status, MAU threshold, or deal-count requirement is publicly documented for the badge itself** — the bar is entirely the designation+specialization stack (which itself embeds revenue/MAU/skilling metrics).
- **Where it shows:** Partners meeting requirements find the badge in **Logo Builder** (aka.ms/logobuilder, MAICPP Admin access). [MS-OFFICIAL: Aug 2026 Partner Center announcements https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/partner-center/announcements/2026-august]
- **What it unlocks:** Primarily differentiation/marketing (badge + certified letter) and field-sales identification; press frames it as signaling capability to take customers "past AI pilots into governed, enterprise-scale production." No published incentive attaches to the badge per se (incentives attach to the underlying specializations). [CHANNEL-PRESS: HSO, Insight]
- **Retirement:** Announced at **MCAPS Start for Partners, July 2026**: badge **retires end of June 2027**. Holders may use it through FY27 while continuing to meet requirements; partners can still newly attain it in FY27; after June 2027 nobody may use it. [MS-OFFICIAL: Aug 2026 Partner Center announcements, "Frontier Partner badge retirement and Frontier Partner specialization transition"]
- **Known holders (press):** Accenture/Avanade, Slalom, HSO, Insight, Tietoevry, Protiviti. Total count: not disclosed. [CHANNEL-PRESS]

## 2. Frontier Partner SPECIALIZATION (FY27, coming)

- **Official state:** "Available later in FY27" in Partner Center; described as expanding on the badge's capabilities; positioned in channel press as "the highest distinction in the partner ecosystem — spanning Copilot, Foundry, Fabric and Security"; announced by CPO Nicole Dezen with the 2026 "State of the Partner" / April 2026 Frontier Transformation blog; purpose = "a clear way for customers and Microsoft field sales teams to identify partners with validated readiness" for agentic AI. [MS-OFFICIAL: https://techcommunity.microsoft.com/blog/specialization-blog/coming-soon-the-frontier-partner-specialization/4540152 (renders title + timeline only); https://blogs.microsoft.com/blog/2026/04/21/accelerating-frontier-transformation-with-microsoft-partners/ ; aka.ms/FrontierPartnerCollection linked from Aug 2026 announcements]
- **Reported requirements (NOT yet in official docs — treat as provisional):** hold **four prerequisite specializations simultaneously under one Partner Global Account**: (1) Microsoft 365 Copilot, (2) AI Apps on Azure OR AI Platform on Azure, (3) Data Security, (4) Identity and Access Management; PLUS (per Softspend's decode) **5 team members with Frontier Transformation Engineer badges**, **3 with Fabric Analytics Engineer Associate (DP-600)**, and a **third-party audit** validating delivery across design/build/deploy/govern/secure, **revalidated every two years**. [CHANNEL-PRESS: https://softspend.com/community/post/microsoft-fy27-strategy-decoded-for-microsoft-365-partners — explicitly "subject to Microsoft's final FY27 rules"; four-prereq list corroborated by MCAPS FY27 coverage (TD SYNNEX Amplify, Alif)]
- **Reported benefits:** Frontier-aligned co-sell opportunities, Microsoft **Agent prepurchase plan credits**, **Microsoft 365 E7 licensing**/product benefits, packaged GTM resources, priority visibility, **MAICPP Concierge support**. [CHANNEL-PRESS: same sources]
- **Audit model:** consistent with Microsoft's 2026 pivot — all four Security specializations moved to partner-funded third-party audits every 2 years (live July 30, 2026), and the M365 Copilot specialization replaced customer references with a third-party capabilities audit — so a third-party audit for Frontier is highly plausible and reported, but unconfirmed officially. [MS-OFFICIAL for the audit pivot: Aug 2026 announcements]

## 3. Copilot partner tier ladder — "Copilot Jumpstart"

- **Program:** **Copilot Jumpstart Partner Initiative** (FY25-origin, invite/nomination-flavored recognition program, separate from MAICPP specializations). Tiers, bottom→top:
  1. **Community** (entry level) — [CHANNEL-PRESS: CBTS "advanced to Ready from the Community level" https://www.cbts.com/newsroom/cbts-achieves-microsoft-365-copilot-jumpstart-ready-tier-designation]
  2. **Ready** — advanced delivery maturity across advisory, deployment, adoption; attained via a **Microsoft "Ready Tier" audit** reviewing "procedures, documentation, and ROI presentations" (CBTS); press cites prerequisites like being a certified Modern Work partner + Change & Adoption-type credentials, internal Copilot rollout, secure-deployment capability. [CHANNEL-PRESS: CBTS, Epiq, Easi, Crimson, kom4tec, Centric]
  3. **Prioritized/Prioritised** — "highest level of recognition"; vetted+validated Copilot practice, proven results, early enablement investment, successful internal rollout; positions partner as "hero partner" for field sellers. [CHANNEL-PRESS: https://www.increment.inc/post/prioritised-tier-microsoft-copilot-jumpstart-partner-program ; Invoke, CTG press]
  - No official public Microsoft page enumerates the tiers; program docs are gated (FastTrack Partner Community). "Advanced" as a distinct tier: not verified.
- **What tiers unlock:**
  - **Ready+:** approved Copilot partner status, engagement funding, workshops with Microsoft, FastTrack deployment vouchers, Copilot/Power Platform project funding. [CHANNEL-PRESS: kom4tec, Centric]
  - **FY27 M365 Copilot Voucher Program:** only **Copilot Jumpstart (Ready Tier and above)** partners who accepted the M365 Copilot Offer T&Cs may combine the M365 Copilot Offer with the M365 E3/E5/E7 Migration & Deployment Offer. Voucher window: purchases Jul 1, 2026–Jun 30, 2027; redeem within 6 months; customer must buy **≥500 seats of M365 Copilot** (enterprise, 500+ paid seats); pay = lesser of voucher value vs SOW; commercial = 50% at SOW approval + 50% at POE; public sector = 100% at completion; US Federal/GPP/CSP customers excluded. [MS-OFFICIAL (gated KB, rendered): https://fpc.microsoft.com/knowledgebase/article/KB-01831/en-us] — Note: a separate snippet pass on KB-01831 surfaced "at least 200 seats"; the fuller render says 500. Conflict flagged; 500 is the better-attested figure and matches the control-before-scale dossier.
  - **Frontier Accelerate for Copilot (MCI) eligibility bridge:** "Partners who are **Copilot Jumpstart Ready or higher tier as of June 30, 2026** will be eligible between **July 1, 2026 through December 31, 2026**; **Copilot Specialization required effective January 1, 2027**." Eligibility can be one of: Copilot Specialization / Copilot Jumpstart Ready+ / Business Applications specialization (Agent Solution Deployment Accelerator only). [CHANNEL-PRESS: Crayon APAC FY27 Partner Briefing deck, p.24, https://apac.crayonchannel.com/app/uploads/2026/07/Slides_-Webinar-FY27-Microsoft-Partner-Briefing.pdf ; corroborated by Softspend: "JumpStart Ready+ ceases being honored after that date"] — this strengthens (still not [MS-OFFICIAL]) the Jan 1, 2027 gate flagged [UNVERIFIED] in control-before-scale/04.
- **The ladder is being deprecated as an incentive gate:** from Jan 1, 2027 the **Microsoft 365 Copilot specialization** is the gate; Jumpstart tiers remain a recognition/voucher construct.
- **How a brand-new SMB partner climbs (FY27 mechanics):**
  - Entry motion without any tier: **Copilot in 30** — CSP partner-led 25-user/30-day M365 Copilot Business trial for <300-employee customers, in CSP New Commerce Aug 1–Dec 31, 2026 (product CFQ7TTC0MM8R, SKU 006Z), plus launch kit + free Copilot Success Planner (aka.ms/CopilotIn30). Permanent SKUs "M365 Business Standard/Premium with Copilot" from Jul 1, 2026. [MS-OFFICIAL: Aug + June 2026 Partner Center announcements]
  - Revenue accelerators need no tier: CSP Strategic Product Accelerator Tier 1 (BP, E3, Copilot Business, bundles, Defender/Purview suites) 2.5% flat; Tier 2 (E5/E7/Agent 365/M365 Copilot/Copilot Studio) 7.0%; Growth Accelerator 12.5% indirect reseller / 10% direct (Oct 1, 2026); "up to 19.5%" Modern Work stack. [CHANNEL-PRESS: Crayon deck pp.9–10; Cloud Factory]
  - MCI engagement funding needs Jumpstart Ready+ (through Dec 31, 2026) then M365 Copilot specialization (Jan 1, 2027+) — so the realistic SMB path is: designation → specialization (see §5), using Jumpstart only if already invited/audited.

## 4. Frontier DISTRIBUTOR designation

- **What:** designation for CSP **distributors** with advanced distribution capabilities across cloud/AI/security; announced at Ignite Nov 2025; updated April 2026 to reflect agentic-AI scaling (skilling, agent-management enablement, Marketplace-backed motions). [CHANNEL-PRESS: Sherweb Ignite recap; MS-OFFICIAL: April 2026 Microsoft blog]
- **Requirements:** active CSP distributor authorization; **Distributor Capability Score: meet 33 of 37 metrics** across six categories (GTM/Sales, Operational Readiness, Platform, Security, Support, Reseller Enablement); **third-party assessment** (qualitative); per Alif also a case-rate evaluation (escalations vs CSP business size) and CSAT audit, with **Support Services designation** attainable separately or bundled (CloudCockpit disputes it being a hard prerequisite — conflict noted); annual renewal, not permanent; not required to keep distributor authorization. Manual enrollment from Nov 2025; Partner Center enrollment expected H2 FY26. [CHANNEL-PRESS: https://cloudcockpit.com/blog/microsoft-frontier-distributor-support-services-designation ; https://www.alifconsulting.com/post/microsoft-frontier-distributor-designation]
- **Benefits:** up to **$24,000 Azure credits**, up to **200 internal-use licenses** (42 products), migration funding, support-plan discounts, **EA-to-CSP for Azure transfer tool** access (Frontier Distributors are a named access class in the July 2026 EA-to-CSP expansion [MS-OFFICIAL: July 2026 Partner Center announcements]), badging. [CHANNEL-PRESS]
- **For resellers underneath:** signals the distributor can support Frontier Transformation motions (agent skilling, marketplace, EA→CSP migrations); reseller-facing badging aids distributor selection; known holders: TD SYNNEX, Dicker Data. [CHANNEL-PRESS: TD SYNNEX Amplify; techpartner.news]
- **Watch item:** Change-of-Channel-Partner (COCP) rules — an indirect reseller switching distributors triggers COCP: **12-month ineligibility for Core/Strategic Accelerator incentives on moved subscriptions**. [CHANNEL-PRESS: Crayon deck p.45] Pick the distributor carefully the first time.

## 5. Relationship map & SMB sequencing

- **Hierarchy (FY27):** Solutions Partner designations (customer-facing badges renamed Aug 2026: Business Applications + Modern Work → **AI Business Solutions**; Data & AI + Digital & App Innovation + Infrastructure → **Cloud & AI Platforms**; Security unchanged; attainment paths/metrics unchanged) → specializations sit on top of designations → **Frontier Partner badge** = 3 designations + 3 specializations → **Frontier Partner specialization** = 4 specializations + engineering badges + audit (highest bar). [MS-OFFICIAL: Aug 2026 announcements (renames + "path to attainment doesn't change")]
- **Microsoft 365 Copilot specialization** (renamed from "Microsoft Copilot" July 2026): prerequisite = Solutions Partner designation in Business Applications/AI Business Solutions, Modern Work, or Security; performance now **paid M365 Copilot MAU growth only** (pre-update bar was 1,000 MAU growth trailing 12 months + 5 net new Copilot customers via CPOR/CSP T1/T2 — post-update official numbers not republished); skilling refreshed (MS-102 & APL-4002/APL-7008 out; **AB-100 Agentic AI Business Solutions Architect**, **AB-620 AI Agent Builder Associate**, SC-401 in); customer references replaced by **third-party capabilities audit valid 2 years**. [MS-OFFICIAL: June 2026 Partner Center announcements; CHANNEL-PRESS numbers: inforcer.com, Sherweb]
- **Agentic Business Solutions specialization** (created Jul 31, 2026 from Low Code + Intelligent Automation merger): performance Pathway One = 2 unique new-deployment customers (1 Power Automate flow in production + 1 Power Apps app ≥5 users, PAL) OR Pathway Two = **2 new Copilot Studio customer deployments ≥$10,000 TTM each**; skilling = 5 people (PL-200/Intelligent App Builder/AI Agent Builder) + 2 people (PL-400 or PL-500) + **2 people AB-100**; plus ≥1 Marketplace consulting offer. It feeds the AI Business Solutions designation track and the Agent Solution Deployment Accelerator, but is NOT in the Frontier badge/spec prerequisite lists. Also coming: **Agentic Security specialization** (FY27, in design). [MS-OFFICIAL: Aug 2026 announcements]
- **Realistic small-partner sequencing:** (1) SMB-track Solutions Partner designation (Modern Work or Security — SMB tracks exist; July 2026 change removed the ACR floor on Azure-path SMB tracks, ≥80% SMB/SMC-Corporate customers) → (2) **Microsoft 365 Copilot specialization** (drive paid MAU via Copilot in 30 / Copilot Business bundles; pass audit) → (3) add Data Security + IAM specializations (both now audit-based) → (4) AI Apps or AI Platform on Azure specialization (Azure ACR-class bars) → (5) **Frontier Partner specialization**. Steps 3–5 are realistically multi-year for a small partner; the near-term payoff point is step 2 (the Jan 1, 2027 incentive gate).
