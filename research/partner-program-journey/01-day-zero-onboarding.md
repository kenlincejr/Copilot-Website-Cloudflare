## 1. Enrollment from absolute zero

**Free membership exists.** Base MAICPP enrollment has no fee; it grants Partner Center access, training resources, marketing resources, and eligibility to pursue paid packages/designations. [MS-OFFICIAL — implied by enrollment docs listing no fee, https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/partner-center/enroll/partner-center-enroll-overview, updated 2026-07-28; corroborated by partner.microsoft.com "no cost to enroll" via search snippet] [CHANNEL-PRESS corroboration: aicloudpartners.com guide]

**Exact enrollment steps** [MS-OFFICIAL, enroll overview page above]:

1. **Initiate**: partner.microsoft.com → "join the program" → select **Partner** → Next. (Direct URL: partner.microsoft.com/dashboard/account/v3/enrollment/introduction/partnership)
2. **Work account**: Log in with an existing Entra work account or use "Create work account" — this creates a tenant **and an organization billing account that serves as the legal entity**. Explicitly disallowed: personal Azure free/trial accounts, Azure Government tenants, legacy EA-only constructs, MSA/Hotmail/Outlook.com accounts, government domains, free/disposable email domains, and generic role addresses (info@, admin@). If you have no business email domain, you must buy one first. [MS-OFFICIAL, same page + https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/partner-center/enroll/enroll-as-indirect-reseller]
3. **Submit enrollment**: sign in, select billing account/legal entity, read + accept the MAICPP Agreement, click Enroll. Some countries require a Registration ID. The enrollee must be **Global admin** and have authority to sign agreements and produce legal documents.
4. **Verification**: monitor at Account Settings → Legal info. Enrollment is **Active only once vetting = Authorized**.

**Verification detail** [MS-OFFICIAL, https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/partner-center/enroll/understand-the-verification-process, updated 2026-07-28]:

- Five independent checks: **email verification** (business email of primary contact), **identity verification** (government-issued ID of at least one user; name must match), **employment verification** (domain-registrar documentation showing business name/address/domains/purchase+expiry dates), **business verification** (formation docs — articles/certificate of incorporation, business license — matching registration records exactly, no abbreviations), plus discretionary **additional verification** (trust questionnaire).
- **Typical duration: 3–5 business days**, longer if extra review needed. [MS-OFFICIAL; also in benefits FAQ]
- Until verified, purchasing offers/publishing/managing settings is blocked. Statuses: Authorized / Pending / Rejected (appeal path exists: submit-verification-appeal).

**Partner IDs** [MS-OFFICIAL, enroll overview]: Enrollment auto-creates two IDs — a **Partner Global Account (PGA)** and a **Partner Location Account (PLA)**. Look up at Account Settings → Identifiers. These are the modern successors to MPN IDs (the term "PartnerID" is used throughout; CSP requires a PLA ID of type *legal entity* matching the transaction country). No August-2026 official reference to a "PartnerOne ID" was found — see could-not-verify list.

**Post-enrollment next actions** [MS-OFFICIAL]: assign workspace roles (users must be in the Entra tenant), verify notification email, then Membership workspace → view offers.

## 2. Paid benefits packages and current prices (Action Pack successors)

Action Pack + Learning Pack retired **January 21/22, 2025**; Microsoft's named successors are the Partner Success packages. [CHANNEL-PRESS: theregister.com/2024/08/28/microsoft_action_pack_2025/, techradar; MS-OFFICIAL: learn.microsoft.com/en-us/partner-center/membership/mpn-get-action-pack]

All three packages: annual, one purchase per PGA (no exceptions), 30-day cancellation window if nothing redeemed, membership 365 days + 30-day renewal window on benefits. Prices exclude tax. [MS-OFFICIAL, package pages below]

**Recommendation logic for an SMB-focused startup partner** (from the data above): Launch $350 is the entry point (one-time tier; can only be bought once ever per PGA); Core $925 is the Action Pack analog (adds support incidents + advisory hours + $2,400 Azure); Expanded $4,125 is the **only package with M365 Copilot seats**. Direction of guidance in Microsoft's own docs: Launch = "entry point for Services Partners," Core = "create, build, expand," Expanded = "scale further."

**Redemption mechanics change**: New guided $0-purchase redemption flow tied to the MCA billing account (no more key-based activation); benefits redeemable only on tenants under that billing account. Effective **Nov 1, 2026**, benefit expiration aligns to package purchase date. Renewal window tightens to AD-30 (renew by anniversary date). [MS-OFFICIAL, https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/partner-center/announcements/2026-august]

## 3. Solutions Partner designation fee & benefits

- **Current annual fee: $4,875 USD** + applicable taxes (raised from the legacy-Gold-equivalent $4,730). One fee covers **all** designations you qualify for — subsequent designations at no additional cost. Purchasable only after meeting the partner capability score requirements (70+ points). [MS-OFFICIAL, https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/partner-center/membership/mpn-pay-fee-silver-gold-competency, updated 2026-04-09: "The current annual price for the Solution Partner Designation program is $4,875 USD"] — **this verifies the playbook's F-014 figure.**
- Historical context ($1,670 Silver / $4,730 Gold → $4,730 flat at 2022 launch): [CHANNEL-PRESS/COMMUNITY: cspcontrolcenter.com, mavencollectivemarketing.com]
- **Common designation benefits** (any designation) [MS-OFFICIAL, benefits-at-a-glance, guide dated 2026-02-18]: **50 hrs** presales/deployment advisory + unlimited technical sales prep, **50 cloud support incidents**, 20 on-prem support incidents, Partner Marketing Center Pro, 50 Concierge units, customer-facing badges.
- **Designation-specific additive benefits** (headline examples): Modern Work: **200× M365 E5** (no Teams), 20× M365 Copilot, 25k Copilot Credits/mo, $4,000 Azure. Business Applications: 100-seat D365 stack, 20× M365 Copilot, 100k Copilot Credits/mo, $4,000 Azure. The three Azure designations (Data & AI / Digital & App Innovation / Infrastructure): **$10,000 Azure credits/yr each** + $5,000/yr GitHub Copilot Enterprise + a shared 100× M365 E3 core set granted once. Security: 100× M365 E5, $10,000 Azure, $5,000/yr Security Copilot credits. Specializations add more (e.g., Azure specialization: $12,000 Azure credits; caps apply). [MS-OFFICIAL, same page]
- **Badge renaming effective Aug 2026**: customer-facing badges consolidate to three commercial solution areas — *Solutions Partner for AI Business Solutions* (Business Applications + Modern Work), *Solutions Partner for Cloud & AI Platforms* (3 Azure paths), *Solutions Partner for Security*. The six solution paths, scoring, fees, benefits unchanged. [MS-OFFICIAL, announcements/2026-august, dated Aug 13, 2026]

## 4. CSP program enrollment

### Indirect reseller (the practical path for a new SMB partner)

Steps [MS-OFFICIAL, https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/partner-center/enroll/enroll-as-indirect-reseller, updated 2026-06-25]:

1. Prereqs: **Active MAICPP enrollment (vetting Authorized)**; location PartnerID of type *legal entity* matching the CSP country; business email; Global Admin; Microsoft **recommends a separate tenant for CSP** vs the MAICPP tenant (tenant isolation).
2. Enroll at the CSP Indirect Reseller enrollment page (aka.ms/accounts/varEnrollment); select/create Billing Account ID; associate location PartnerID; provide **customer support email + phone** (mandatory — proof you can support customers).
3. Business verification (same process as §1).
4. **Sign the Microsoft Partner Agreement (MPA)** — Global Admin only, once per CSP region; cannot be accepted on your behalf by support or a distributor. Enrollment activates only after MPA acceptance.
5. **Establish a distributor relationship** — required to transact; authorization alone is insufficient. The distributor (indirect provider — e.g., TD SYNNEX, Pax8, Ingram Micro) sends a partnership request in Partner Center; reseller accepts. Find one via partner.microsoft.com/membership/cloud-solution-provider/find-a-provider. Distributors typically run their own **credit/financing terms** onboarding. [MS-OFFICIAL for mechanism; distributor credit checks are their own process — CHANNEL-PRESS/common practice]

Ongoing indirect reseller requirements (assessed annually, per PLA, on CSP onboard anniversary month) [MS-OFFICIAL, https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/partner-center/enroll/indirect-reseller-eligibility-requirements, updated 2026-06-25]:

- Active MAICPP membership + PLA in region
- **≥ USD $1,000 CSP transactional TTM revenue at the PLA level** (perpetual software counts; CPOR/DPOR/PAL excluded)
- Mandatory security score items (MFA for all admins, security contact)
- Signed MPA
- Warnings at T-90/T-60/T-30; 30-day cure after anniversary, then deauthorization.

### Direct bill

[MS-OFFICIAL, https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/partner-center/enroll/direct-partner-new-requirements, updated 2026-05-18]:

- **Must transact as an indirect reseller ≥12 months before applying.**
- **Revenue: ≥ USD $1,000,000 eligible CSP TTM revenue at the PGA level.** ⚠ The old **$300K** threshold is obsolete — raised to $1M with FY26 reviews from Oct 1, 2025. [MS-OFFICIAL + CHANNEL-PRESS: cspcontrolcenter.com, kaseya.com]
  - ⚠ **Two live Microsoft pages contradict each other on whether indirect-reseller revenue counts** (found in dossier 05 research): `enroll/csp-overview` (updated 2026-06-25) says "$1M in CSP trailing 12 months transactional revenue at the PGA level… **Indirect Reseller revenue is excluded**," while `enroll/direct-bill-eligibility-requirements` (updated 2026-08-26) says "at least USD $1 million in eligible CSP revenue **as an Indirect Reseller** at the PGA level." These are mutually exclusive. A partner cannot determine from public docs which applies — **confirm with the PDM before planning a direct-bill transition.** The same page also still labels its requirements table "FY26" two months into FY27.
- **Support plan: ASfP or Premier/PSfP required** — but purchase it only *after* passing credit check, as the final step before MPA acceptance. ASfP list price **from ~$16,500/yr** [CHANNEL-PRESS: uscloud.com; MS pricing page partner.microsoft.com/support/advanced-cloud-support is bot-blocked — price not confirmed from MS directly].
- **At least one active Solutions Partner designation** (i.e., + $4,875/yr).
- Full security score compliance (MFA, security contact, 24-hr alert response), assessed at PGA level; from Aug 2026, MAICPP enrollment + security validated per-tenant with enforcement starting 90 days after release. [MS-OFFICIAL, announcements/2026-august]
- Application via external form (mspa-prodcrmcdb.powerappsportals.com) → automated capability assessment (sell/secure/support scoring) → **business verification + company credit check** → email approval → buy support plan → accept MPA → tenant activated.
- Annual reassessment; failure ⇒ forced transition to indirect reseller with **one-year wait** to reapply.

## 5. FY27 membership changes

- **MAICPP Agreement update effective September 1, 2026**: updated agreement posted for review July 1, 2026; terms take effect **automatically** Sept 1, 2026 — no signature or acknowledgement required. Preview PDF: assetsprod.microsoft.com/maicpp-agreement-preview.pdf. [MS-OFFICIAL, https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/partner-center/announcements/2026-august, announcement dated Aug 13, 2026]
- **New benefits packages coming "early FY27"**: announced at MCAPS Start for Partners (July 22, 2026) — new partner benefits packages will add **Microsoft 365 E7, Agent 365, Microsoft 365 Copilot Business, Defender and Purview for Business Premium Suites, and Dragon Copilot seats**, with a unified Partner Center activation experience. Quantities/pricing not yet published. [CHANNEL-PRESS: rcpmag.com/blogs/rcp-channel-briefing/2026/07/microsoft-outlines-fy27-priorities.aspx; MS-OFFICIAL primary (partner.microsoft.com/blog/article/mcaps-start-for-partners-2026 and techcommunity post 4544439) is bot-blocked]
- **"Frontier" is not a membership package** — it's a badge/specialization: the **Frontier Partner badge retires end of June 2027**; attainable and usable through FY27; replaced by the **Frontier Partner specialization** ("available in Partner Center soon in FY27"), validating agent design/build/deploy/govern/secure capability, with co-sell and Microsoft Agent prepurchase benefits. "Frontier Accelerate" is a customer-value/incentive framework, and "Frontier Transformation" the FY27 strategy umbrella. [MS-OFFICIAL: announcements/2026-august + techcommunity.microsoft.com/blog/specialization-blog/coming-soon-the-frontier-partner-specialization/4540152] See dossier 02.
- Related FY27 context: M365 E7 GA May 1, 2026 at ~$99/user/mo, E7 promos (10–15% off) retire Oct 1, 2026 (transact by Sept 30); Agent 365 ~$15/user/mo requiring E5/A5/Business Premium; Copilot Business promos extended through year-end, Business Basic + Copilot Business bundle ~$21/user/mo; CSP incentives shift from flat rebates to growth margin (launching Oct 1, 2026). [MS-OFFICIAL announcements + CHANNEL-PRESS: thewinningcsp.com, techradar, work365apps/sherweb]
- New/merged specializations Aug 1, 2026: Analytics, Agentic Business Solutions, App Modernization consolidations; FY27 additions: Agentic Security, Digital Sovereignty, Clinical Applications. [CHANNEL-PRESS: thewinningcsp.com, rcpmag]
- France e-Invoicing mandate also lands Sept 1, 2026 (Tax ID accuracy in Partner Center) — unrelated to the agreement update but same date; don't conflate. [MS-OFFICIAL, announcements/2026-july]
