## 1. Verdict

**Do not use in front of a customer until #1–#4 land; after that one-to-two-day punch list it is ship-with-fixes** — the core arithmetic reproduces to the cent, the reseller/PEC firewall survived every attack, and the no-benefit copy discipline is intact, but today the printed customer leave-behind hands the customer the partner's PEC economics and meter markup, the "measured ±15%" claim is forgeable in one click, and the engine can be made to throw or print `$∞` on customer-facing cards.

**The meta-finding:** the file says `VERSION 2.0.0`, but of the v2 spec's six features only F5a/F5b shipped. F1 (ramp), F2 (break-even), F3 (controls), F4 (shadow AI), F5c (markup warning), F5d (MACC), and F6 (calibration guard + capture sheet) are all absent — and F1 and F6 were specced as *live defects*, both of which this run confirmed still shipping (#2, #4).

## 3. What was attacked and held (verified)

- **Core math:** stock defaults reproduce **755,000 credits / $7,550/month to the cent**, by hand and by node — given the shipped seats 26/13/6/7 (see #10 for the label). Funnel correct: 80 licensed (40 Standard excluded), round(0.65×80) = 52 active.
- **Annualization:** single-sourced — every annualizing block (cards ×12/×36, P3 sizing, risk block, break-even low, ECHO, partner-cost) reads the same ctx chain; the one site outside `compute()` agrees. (Whether flat ×12 is the right *model* is #5.)
- **Effort ladder:** step = ((15/0.70)/(1200/125))^¼ = 1.222308; all nine tier×level prices inside $0.70–$15.00; per-user inside $82–$228 for **all ten presets at all three levels** (worst: MSP@High $211.08). The retired Extra High/Max levels genuinely broke both bounds ($17.93/$21.91 per task; $258/$315 per user) — correctly removed. High escapes the per-user range only under hand-built mixes no preset reaches, and V-09 fires every time. Legacy xhigh/max sessions land on High with V-19 *in the engine* (but see #19 — the loader kills it).
- **Bands:** symmetric `×(1−b)/×(1+b)`, single-sourced from `CONST.BAND`, identical at all seven render sites.
