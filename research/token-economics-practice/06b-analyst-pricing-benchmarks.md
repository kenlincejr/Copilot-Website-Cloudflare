# Analyst and industry pricing benchmarks for AI managed services, October 2026

**Gathered:** 2026-10-12. **Status:** research-agent memo. Gartner, Forrester, IDC, TSIA and Canalys reports are paywalled, so almost every figure is SECONDARY (press or blogs citing the analyst). Only the FinOps Foundation adoption figure was read on the primary page. Distributors excluded.

## Headline
**No analyst or industry body publishes a "$ per managed agent per month" benchmark**, or a fee-as-percent-of-AI-spend norm. The usable anchors are per-user AI add-ons, governance retainers, project fees and vendor outcome prices.

## Pricing-model direction
- **Gartner:** spending shifts from seats to usage, agent and outcome pricing.
- **TSIA:** three stages, seat to value-based consumption to outcome. The "cannibalization dilemma": agents shrink seat revenue unless a new unit replaces it.
- **MSP bodies:** a monetization gap. Nearly every MSP offers AI, few earn meaningful revenue from it, and no standard price has formed.

## Do not publish without a primary source
- The 73% overrun and 18% budget-share figures.
- The Gartner "2x over budget by 2027" figure relayed in memo 06.
- Any vendor outcome price until read on the vendor's own pricing page.
