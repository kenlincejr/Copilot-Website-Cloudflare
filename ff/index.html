Draft in your league's points, not somebody else's.

Every free draft tool ranks players by consensus ADP. ADP encodes standard scoring. If your league pays for receptions, 40-yard plays, return yards, or uses anything but default defensive tiers, that board is quietly wrong — and it's wrong in the same direction for all twelve of you.

Draftline takes a stat-level projection for all 267 draftable players, runs it back through your rules, and drafts off that. It runs entirely in your browser: no server, no sync, nothing to go down at 7:01pm.

Value over replacement, derived

Replacement level comes from your roster shape and team count, never a hardcoded list. Twelve teams starting one tight end puts replacement at TE13; change the roster and every number on the board moves with it.

Survival, not vibes

Every player carries a real ADP standard deviation from ~7,800 mock drafts. When you pick back-to-back at the turn, the board tells you who is actually likely to still be there — the difference between a reach and a free roll.

Off the board in one click

Type a name, hit Enter. Someone else took him. Shift+Enter means you did. Ctrl+Z undoes any depth. The list re-ranks itself while the clock runs.
