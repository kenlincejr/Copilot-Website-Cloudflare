The whole draft, computed while you're on the clock.

Draftline is a full draft room in your browser: every player scored in your league's rules, a board that re-ranks itself on every pick, and odds — from real draft results — on who is still going to be there when your turn comes back around.

  • Real drafts, not one average rank. Every player carries the spread of where he actually went across roughly 7,800 mock drafts, so the board answers in probabilities instead of a ranking.
  • Claude reads the board with you. A briefing lands before your clock starts, written against the live board and what the teams ahead of you still need.
  • A graded report at the end. All twelve rosters scored in your own rules and ranked, then a written read on yours.

The account lives on Draftline's server, so the same name and password work from any device and your draft follows you between them. There is no email on file, which means there is no password reset — write it down. The board itself still runs entirely in this browser and keeps working if the connection drops.
