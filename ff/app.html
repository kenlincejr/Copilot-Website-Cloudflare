Keepers

A keeper removes the player from the pool and burns that round's pick for whoever owns him. Add opponents' keepers too if you know them — it changes who is on the clock.

Save / load

Your draft autosaves to this browser on every pick. Export writes the whole thing — league rules, keepers, every pick — to a file you can carry to another machine.
