Line04The shift

Why the same work pays twice as long.

An agent that isn’t actively managed degrades — hallucinations creep in, grounding goes stale, permissions drift, usage drops, and the customer quietly concludes AI “didn’t work.” The recurring work is the product.

The motion most partners run today
Hunt for a SOW→Build on T&M→Invoice→Cut bait

Revenue stops. The agent drifts.

The customer blames AI — and you inherit the cleanup at whatever margin the market sets.

The motion this playbook builds
Build the agent→Hand to AgentCare→Retainer begins→Repeat

Revenue compounds. The agent stays healthy.

Every agent you ship creates an obligation to manage it. That obligation is your retainer — and it never ends, because the agents never stop running.

The one rule that protects your margin

Agent builds are always scoped and billed separately as project work — $3,500–$8,000+ per agent. AgentCare covers ongoing management of deployed agents only, never new construction. Every new agent request is a revenue event, not a scope-creep conversation.

Worked example: a 50-user customer with 3 agents at Tier 2 pays ~$1,725/mo. Ship three more agents — no change to users or services — and MRR moves to ~$2,775/mo automatically.

Line10Monday

Four moves, in this order. Not in parallel.

Build capability privately, name the person who owns it, land customers with a low-risk first engagement, then make the recurring offer explicit.

Move 1 · this week
Be Customer Zero

Every tech, AM, and seller on Copilot and Copilot Studio. Build two or three internal agents you actually use. Your most credible demo is your own workflow.

Stanford: 61% of successful deployments had a prior failed one. Fail on your own tenant.
Move 2 · this quarter
Name the AI lead — with authority

Probably not a new hire. Formalize it: title, budget line, and the right to say “no, we’re not doing that engagement.”

Sponsorship is about actions, not approval.
Move 3 · the land motion
Lead with a posture assessment

A productized 1–2 week engagement at $3K–$10K: SharePoint permissions, shadow-AI inventory, tenant readiness, remediation roadmap. Not a license sale.

Field waterfall: assessment → remediation → deployment → $50K–$100K+ LTV.
Move 4 · before quarter end
Name it and price it
