Start here: what kind of plan is this?

This planner prices Copilot Credits the way Microsoft bills them. Cowork and Copilot Studio agents draw from one pool of credits in the customer’s tenant, so the planner sizes them together, finds the cheapest way to buy the pool, and writes the settings that keep it from running out. Every number links to Microsoft’s published rate, and every field you do not have in the room keeps a sensible default. Pick the plan type that matches the conversation you are having; you can change it at any time without losing anything you have entered.

  1. Size itAnswer the Cowork questions, or describe each agent: who uses it, how often, and what it does.
  2. Pool and buyThe planner adds everything into one monthly pool and tests every way to buy it: capacity packs, a pre-purchase plan and pay-as-you-go.
  3. Leave behindPrint the one-page customer report and the guardrail settings sheet, copy the quote text for your provider, and save the session file.

Nothing you enter here is saved, stored or transmitted anywhere: not to a server, not to browser storage, and no analytics are loaded. Everything lives in this browser tab’s memory. The Cowork tab is the Cowork Cost & Conversation Simulator running inside this page; it hands its customer-safe figures (credits by month, its band, seats by license and its pricing basis) to the other tabs through a same-tab browser message and same-origin page access, inside this browser tab only. Margins, provider rates and service prices never leave the partner layer. Save your work with Save session; the file is the only memory this tool has, and no link carries your inputs.

Microsoft rates are as of the rate card dated 2026-10-11, each linked to its Microsoft source with that source’s date. Re-check them after Ignite (November 17–20, 2026). Every figure carries a label: Microsoft is published by Microsoft and linked; Modeled applies Microsoft’s method to assumptions on this page; Estimate is a planner placeholder for something Microsoft does not publish; You said is your input; Measured comes from the customer’s own usage; Likely marks a rule applied as the most probable reading where Microsoft has not said it in one sentence. Partner service prices are Copilot Playbook starting points for you to edit. For the engagement around these numbers, see the Copilot Studio agent engagement guide and An Agent Is Never Done.
